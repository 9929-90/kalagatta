## Event basics

Share wedding date, venue, function name, start time, expected duration, guest count, and language preference. Mention whether the event is indoor, outdoor, or spread across multiple spaces.

## Family names

Prepare names of the couple, parents, siblings, grandparents, close relatives, and special guests. Add pronunciation notes where needed.

## Function sequence

List entries, rituals, speeches, performances, games, cake, dinner, DJ, and closing moments. Mark anything that must happen at a fixed time.

## Vendor cues

Share planner, DJ, sound, photographer, choreographer, and venue coordinator contacts. The anchor should know who confirms each cue.

## Games and interaction

Decide whether games are needed. Share comfort level, audience age group, and any topics to avoid.

## Language and tone

Choose Hindi, English, or Hinglish. Mention whether the tone should be royal, emotional, energetic, formal, or family-friendly.

## Backup plan

Decide what the anchor can do if the couple is delayed, a performance is late, or dinner timing changes. A backup plan keeps the event calm.
