## The visible voice of the event

A wedding emcee or anchor is the person guests hear. They introduce entries, performances, games, speeches, rituals, and transitions. Their work is public and directly affects the guest experience.

## The logistics role

An event coordinator or planner manages venue setup, vendor timing, decor, hospitality, artist movement, and backstage logistics. Their work is often less visible but essential.

## Where the roles overlap

The overlap happens around timing. The planner may tell the anchor that the couple is ready for entry. The anchor then prepares the audience and announces the moment. If the photographer needs two minutes, the anchor fills the gap.

## Why both roles help

When both roles are clear, the event feels smooth. The coordinator manages reality behind the scenes. The anchor translates that reality into a polished stage experience.

## What families should do

Introduce the anchor and coordinator before the event. Share one final schedule. Decide who has authority to change sequence. Provide contact numbers. This small step prevents confusion.
