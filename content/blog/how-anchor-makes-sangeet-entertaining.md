## Sangeet is more than performances

A sangeet often includes choreographed dances, family entries, couple moments, games, speeches, and DJ energy. Without an anchor, these pieces can feel disconnected. A professional host gives the evening a clear rhythm.

## Performance introductions

Every performance deserves context. The anchor can introduce the performers, explain the relation to the couple, and create anticipation. This makes the audience more involved than a simple music start.

## Family energy

Sangeet nights work because families participate. The anchor can invite sides of the family, create friendly challenges, and make relatives feel celebrated. The tone should be playful, not competitive in a harsh way.

## Couple storytelling

Short couple stories can make the event more personal. These can include how the couple met, what families love about them, or small habits that make them unique. The anchor should keep it warm and concise.

## Handling gaps

Performances often take time to set up. Costumes, sound cues, lighting, and group positioning can delay the next act. A good anchor keeps guests engaged during these gaps without making the delay obvious.

## Ending the night well

The closing matters. The anchor can thank families, invite everyone for dinner or DJ, and transition the mood smoothly. A strong ending prevents the event from fading awkwardly.
