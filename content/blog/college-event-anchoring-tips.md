## Understand the audience

College events are energetic and unpredictable. Students respond quickly, but they also lose attention quickly. The anchor must keep the pace sharp while respecting faculty, sponsors, and institutional rules.

## Set the tone early

A strong opening tells students that the event will be fun but organized. The anchor should create excitement without making the stage chaotic.

## Respect protocol

Faculty introductions, chief guests, sponsors, and prize distributions need respectful language. Names and designations should be checked in advance.

## Keep transitions quick

College programs often include many performances. Long introductions can slow the event. Short, energetic transitions work better.

## Use clean humor

Humor should never target students personally or cross institutional boundaries. The best college anchors are funny without being careless.

## Handle competitions clearly

If there are competitions, explain rules, judging, time limits, and results clearly. Confusion can reduce audience trust.
