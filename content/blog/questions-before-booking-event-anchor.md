## Start with availability

Ask whether the anchor is available for your date and city. If there are multiple functions, share all dates and approximate timings. Availability for one evening does not automatically mean availability for a full destination wedding.

## Ask about event experience

Ask which event types the anchor commonly hosts. Weddings, corporate events, college festivals, and private parties all need different stage behavior. Request relevant portfolio links or examples.

## Ask about language

Confirm Hindi, English, or bilingual hosting. Also explain the guest profile. A good anchor will suggest a language mix based on elders, friends, corporate guests, and outstation attendees.

## Ask about preparation

Will there be a briefing call? Does the anchor prepare scripts? Are games included? Who shares family names, speaker lists, award categories, or performance order? Preparation should be clear before booking.

## Ask about timing and overtime

Events can start late. Ask how duration is counted and what happens if the program extends. Clear terms prevent awkward conversations on event day.

## Ask about coordination

Find out whether the anchor coordinates with planners, DJs, sound teams, choreographers, and photographers. Stage flow improves when the anchor knows the cue chain.

## Ask about travel

For events outside Udaipur, confirm travel, stay, reporting time, and local transport. Mention venue location early.
